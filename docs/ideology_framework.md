# Ideology Framework

Kaiserreich 2 should preserve the recognizable Kaiserreich ideological spectrum while allowing the post-Second Weltkrieg world to become harsher, more fragmented, and more experimental.

The central rule is separation between state ideology and radical current. A government may be formally conservative, liberal, socialist, or nationalist while also being pressured by underground syndicalists, anarchist militias, totalist cadres, technocratic security planners, or Russian maximalists.

## Recommended Ideology Families

### Social Conservative

Traditional constitutional order, church influence, monarchy or republican respectability, veteran politics, and fear of renewed revolution.

### Market Liberal

Reconstruction capitalism, trade normalization, debt management, foreign investment, and institutional restraint.

### Social Liberal

Constitutional reconciliation, welfare compromise, labor legalization, civil liberties, and anti-authoritarian reconstruction.

### Social Democrat

Democratic labor politics, parliamentary socialism, union legalization, and reformist answers to syndicalist memory.

### Syndicalist

Worker organization, industrial democracy, union power, and revolutionary legitimacy through labor institutions. After the Second Weltkrieg, syndicalism is often underground or semi-legal rather than openly governing.

### Anarchist

A separate ideology from syndicalism. Anarchism rejects state power, party hierarchy, totalist discipline, imperial restoration, and German-led security order. It can appear as urban cells, peasant communes, revolutionary militias, dockworker federations, or anti-colonial guerrilla networks.

Gameplay identity:

- high resistance and sabotage capability,
- weak conventional state capacity,
- strong local legitimacy in certain regions,
- difficult alliance diplomacy,
- vulnerability to repression and fragmentation.

### Totalist

Authoritarian revolutionary socialism shaped by defeat. Totalists argue that decentralized syndicalism failed and that only disciplined party rule, militarized planning, and revolutionary security can defeat reaction.

Gameplay identity:

- strong mobilization and security tools,
- harsh stability tradeoffs,
- easier foreign subversion than open diplomacy,
- rivalry with anarchists and orthodox syndicalists.

### Authoritarian Democrat

Managed constitutionalism, emergency rule, military-backed cabinets, royal prerogative, and security-first reconstruction.

### Paternal Autocrat

Personalist, military, monarchist, clerical, or bureaucratic rule that preserves order through hierarchy rather than mass ideology.

### National Populist

Mass nationalist radicalism rooted in humiliation, ethnic crisis, paramilitary politics, anti-German resentment, anti-syndicalist terror, colonial revolt, or imperial revenge. In Kaiserreich 2, National Populism should be more common than in the 1936 setting because Germany's second victory, Entente restoration, colonial instability, and the defeat of syndicalism all push dissatisfied movements toward national rebirth rather than international revolution.

National Populism should appear frequently in:

- German client states that want protection without obedience,
- Entente colonies and mandates resisting imperial restoration,
- Japanese clients that turn anti-European language against Japanese control,
- Russian-aligned revisionist movements,
- American successor states and borderland militias,
- defeated socialist regions where national humiliation overtakes class politics,
- and colonial officer corps or veterans' leagues promising order through national revival.

## Russian Ideological Space

Russia should not be assigned one universal ideology. Its factional role is revisionist, but its domestic ideology should be path-dependent.

### Constitutional Revisionists

Russia seeks revenge through state modernization, army reform, diplomacy, and controlled parliamentary legitimacy. This is the least radical anti-German path, but still hostile to the Reichspakt settlement.

### Restorationist Monarchists

Russia frames the German order as an offense against dynasty, Orthodoxy, Slavic security, and historical right. This path can cooperate with conservatives abroad but remains threatening to smaller border nations.

### National Maximalists

The most important new Russian radical current. National Maximalists argue that Germany won twice because Russia remained divided, soft, and half-modernized. They call for a mobilized national state, social discipline, military-industrial command, demographic recovery, and total confrontation with Mitteleuropa.

They are not simply generic national populists. They should feel specifically Russian: imperial memory, civil-war trauma, anti-German encirclement, and promises to restore Russia as a civilization rather than merely a state.

### Eurasian Statists

Russia rejects both Western liberalism and German Mitteleuropa, presenting itself as a continental order with its own mission across Slavic, Turkic, Orthodox, and steppe frontiers. This can support a multiethnic imperial framework or a coercive centralizing state.

### Revolutionary Patriots

Former leftists, veterans, and anti-German radicals fuse social revolution with national revenge. They can collaborate with syndicalist remnants abroad while suppressing independent anarchists or unions at home.

## United States Ideological Space

The United States should be modular because the American Civil War can produce very different postwar states. The global systems should read American variables rather than assuming one canon victor.

### Federal Restoration

The constitutional United States survives and rebuilds. It is powerful but cautious, with foreign policy divided between hemispheric defense, anti-syndicalism, Pacific containment of Japan, and suspicion of German dominance.

### Long Reconstruction Republic

The United States reunifies under democratic institutions but remains scarred by emergency powers, regional resentment, and reconstruction bargains. This path is best for a hesitant swing power.

### Populist National State

America turns inward and radical, treating foreign blocs as threats to national renewal. It may oppose syndicalism, German finance, Japanese expansion, and old Entente colonialism at the same time.

### Syndicalist or Socialist America

If a socialist American state survives, it becomes the largest possible refuge for defeated revolutionary politics. It should immediately face internal splits between syndicalists, totalists, anarchists, and democratic socialists.

### Fragmented America

A divided America allows the Third Weltkrieg to begin without a clear western superpower. Regional American states become courted by Germany, Japan, the Entente, Russia, and revolutionary networks.

## Post-German-Victory Radicalization

Germany winning again should not end ideology. It should radicalize it.

- Defeated syndicalists ask whether union democracy was too slow to survive modern war.
- Totalists answer with party discipline and revolutionary militarization.
- Anarchists answer that state socialism reproduced the same coercion it claimed to abolish.
- Russian radicals answer that only a mobilized civilization-state can defeat Germany.
- German hardliners answer that victory proves Europe must be managed more tightly.
- Restored Entente reactionaries answer that compromise with the revolutionary era must end.
- Anti-colonial movements answer that European victory in Europe does not legitimize empire overseas.
- Client-state nationalists answer that German protection has become permanent tutelage.
- Colonial national populists answer that liberation must mean national sovereignty, not new mandates.

## The Postwar Reckoning

The major systemic crisis of the start era should create openings for radical ideologies without making them identical.

- Anarchists grow where state institutions, police legitimacy, and food distribution fail.
- Syndicalists grow where legal labor politics are repressed and industrial workers blame reconstruction capitalism.
- Totalists grow where defeated revolutionaries conclude that only discipline and security can survive reaction.
- National Populists grow where economic pain is interpreted as national humiliation, foreign dependency, or betrayal by client elites.
- Russian maximalists grow where the crisis proves that Germany's order is vulnerable.
- German security technocrats grow where elites fear that ordinary politics cannot preserve the system.

## Implementation Notes

- Add anarchism as a distinct ideology in gameplay, not merely a syndicalist sub-faction.
- Keep syndicalism and totalism mutually suspicious even when aligned against a common enemy.
- Let Russia's international bloc remain revisionist across paths, while domestic ideology changes its methods and rhetoric.
- Use national spirits or scripted variables to track radical currents inside states where they are not the ruling ideology.
- Make ideological splits produce event chains, resistance behavior, foreign volunteers, and faction-cohesion pressure.
- Treat revived nationalism as the primary trigger of the Third Weltkrieg era, with syndicalism acting as an underground accelerant rather than the main global bloc.
