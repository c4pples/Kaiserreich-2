# Postwar Border Changes

This document defines the main border changes from Kaiserreich's 1936 start to the Kaiserreich 2 default start. It is a map-design guide, not yet a state-by-state implementation file.

## Design Principle

The map should show a victorious but overextended German-led settlement, restored but wounded Entente states, a fragmented United States, surviving Russian revisionism, and unresolved colonial pressure. Borders should create future crises rather than permanently solve them.

Germany's victory should mostly appear through preserved influence, treaty rights, demilitarization, basing access, customs ties, and security guarantees. Large new annexations should be rare. The point is that Germany has maintained control of the European system, not that it has conquered Europe outright.

## Europe

### France

Default change:

- The French Republic controls metropolitan France again.
- Alsace-Lorraine remains German.
- The Commune of France no longer exists as a state.

Special zones:

- Northern and eastern France should carry demilitarization, occupation-rights, or inspection mechanics rather than permanent German annexation.
- Paris is restored but politically unstable.
- Former Commune strongholds should have resistance and reconstruction modifiers.

Rationale:

Germany wants France disarmed and safe, not necessarily annexed. France wants sovereignty but lacks the leverage to reject all German security terms.

### Britain

Default change:

- The United Kingdom is restored in Great Britain.
- The Union of Britain no longer exists as a state.
- Ireland remains independent and strategically pressured.

Special zones:

- Major ports can host Entente, Canadian, or treaty inspection rights.
- Industrial regions should retain post-revolutionary unrest.
- Scotland and Wales can have autonomy debates, but full partition should be an alternate path.

Rationale:

Restoration should not mean 1914 Britain returns. The monarchy and Parliament are back, but the social settlement is unresolved.

### Italy

Default change:

- Socialist Italy is defeated.
- Italy should not begin as a perfectly unified, stable great power.

Recommended setup:

- A restored Kingdom or Italian Federation controls the north and Rome.
- The south may be aligned, autonomous, or contested depending on gameplay needs.
- Former socialist regions retain strong underground networks.

Rationale:

Italian unity is too useful as a gameplay question to solve completely before game start.

### Low Countries

Default change:

- Belgium/Flanders-Wallonia settlement should reflect German security priorities.
- The Netherlands survives but is pressured by German trade and British naval interests.

Recommended setup:

- Avoid large annexations unless a specific war outcome justifies them.
- Use military access, guarantees, customs ties, and security missions instead.

### Eastern Europe

Default change:

- Germany's eastern client and allied system survives.
- Poland, Lithuania, Ukraine, Belarus/White Ruthenia, and the Baltic space remain the main fault line with Russia.

Recommended setup:

- Preserve recognizable Reichspakt-aligned eastern states where possible.
- Add border militarization and legitimacy crises.
- Avoid giving Russia major western gains at start unless using an alternate failed-Germany scenario.

Rationale:

The Third Weltkrieg needs an eastern frontier that feels artificial, armed, and politically contested.

### Balkans and Danubia

Default change:

- Austria-Hungary or a successor Danubian order survives as a major regional factor.
- Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, and Albania remain possible crisis actors.

Recommended setup:

- Do not over-consolidate the Balkans into one bloc.
- Use guarantees, claims, and influence rather than total annexation.

## Russia and Borderlands

Default change:

- Russia remains outside the German settlement and has not regained its full imperial frontier.
- It may have recovered some peripheral influence, but not enough to make the Third Weltkrieg already decided.

Recommended flashpoints:

- Ukraine,
- Belarus/White Ruthenia,
- Baltics,
- Caucasus,
- Central Asia,
- Black Sea access.

Rationale:

Russia should begin angry, armed, and incomplete.

## North America

### Former United States

Default change:

- The United States remains fragmented.
- No claimant controls the full prewar USA.

Recommended setup:

- Federal Republic in the East or Northeast.
- Combined Syndicates remnant in the Great Lakes industrial belt.
- American Union or National State in the South.
- Pacific States on the West Coast.
- Contested or neutral interior corridors.

Rationale:

American reunification should be a central campaign, not a completed premise.

### Canada

Default change:

- Canada survives as a major Entente power but is strained by the return of Britain and the American collapse.

Recommended setup:

- No major annexation of American land by default.
- Border security zones, refugee corridors, and influence over New England are better than outright conquest.

### Mexico

Default change:

- Mexico gains strategic importance from American fragmentation.

Recommended setup:

- Possible influence or claims in border regions.
- Avoid excessive default annexations unless tied to a specific American collapse variant.

## Asia-Pacific

### Japan

Default change:

- Japan remains the central Pacific and East Asian power.
- Its exact Chinese settlement should be modular.

Recommended setup:

- Japan controls or dominates key Pacific islands and client networks.
- Avoid giving Japan complete uncontested control of China by default.

### China

Default change:

- China should remain a major variable rather than a solved unitary bloc unless the selected start variant requires it.

Recommended setup:

- A dominant Chinese government may exist, but rivals, concessions, Japanese pressure, and regional militarists should remain.

## Colonies and Mandates

### Africa

Default change:

- Mittelafrika's fate should be modular, but German colonial overstretch remains central.
- Entente restoration creates colonial legitimacy crises.

Recommended setup:

- Use protectorates, mandates, military zones, and insurgencies instead of clean imperial color fills everywhere.

### Middle East

Default change:

- The Ottoman or post-Ottoman order should be selected by start rule.
- Oil, canals, and treaty ports matter more than large clean annexations.

Recommended flashpoints:

- Suez,
- Mesopotamian oil,
- Persia,
- Levant,
- Arabian coast,
- Caucasus approaches.

## Border Change Summary

Required default changes from Kaiserreich:

- Restore the French Republic to metropolitan France.
- Restore the United Kingdom to Great Britain.
- Remove the Commune of France, Union of Britain, and Socialist Italy as normal ruling states.
- Keep Germany's eastern order mostly intact.
- Keep Alsace-Lorraine German.
- Keep Russia incomplete and revisionist.
- Fragment the United States into successor states.
- Preserve Japan as the leading East Asian and Pacific challenger.
- Convert many colonial changes into mandates, influence zones, and insurgency systems rather than simple annexation.
